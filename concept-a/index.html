01 The first fires

Here at
the beginning.

Early Access has begun.
So have we.

Hearthlight is a new guild on Tilustra, starting alongside Monsters & Memories Early Access. The values are clear. The stories, friendships, and traditions are still ahead of us.

The foundation is here.
What gets built on it will be shaped by the people who show up.

Start a group. Suggest an expedition. Share what you’ve learned. Bring an RP idea to life. Joining now is a chance to help make this a place you want to belong.

The founder sets the direction and keeps the guardrails. The life of the guild is something we make together.

02 Out in the world

A reason to log in.
People to set out with.

We’re here to actually play Monsters & Memories together. The people beside you matter alongside whatever you’re taking on.

  • Group up

    Camps, leveling groups, and quests. Learn your classes and the world together.

  • Go deeper

    Dungeon crawls and expeditions. Communicate, take your time, and see what lies ahead.

  • Follow your curiosity

    New roads, rumors, and discoveries. Sometimes the wrong turn is the whole evening.

  • Make and trade

    Crafting, trading, and gearing up. Share what you know and put good finds to use.

  • Lend a hand

    Corpse recovery, a difficult quest, or advice for a new player. Look out for each other.

  • Make an evening of it

    A tavern night, a little RP, or an outing someone wants to organize. Bring an idea and company.

The road is better in good company.
Inhabit the world.

03 A little more character

Live a little
in Eth-ur.

Light-to-medium RP, woven into normal play. A few words in character while grouping. A conversation in town. A dungeon run with an in-world reason. Relationships and stories that grow along the way.

Bring as much character as makes the game more fun for you.

No mandatory RP, backstory, or in-character hours. No long guild storyline to catch up on. There’s room for occasional tavern nights, small hooks, and deeper RP between people who want it.

04 Our ways

Good company takes a little care.

Competent without being sweaty.
A few simple expectations, wherever the road takes us.

  1. 01

    Real life first.

    No attendance requirements and no leveling race. Play when you can; communicate when plans change.

  2. 02

    Be someone people want in the group.

    Learn your character, cooperate, and take feedback well. Things will go sideways. Recover, regroup, learn what you can, and keep moving.

  3. 03

    Take part when you’re here.

    Say hello. Ask for a group. Help someone out or get an outing started. A guild tag should be more than a silent name overhead.

05 Before you set out

A few things
worth knowing.

How big will Hearthlight be?

We’re aiming for around 30–50 members over time, small enough that names stay familiar. People will naturally come and go; perhaps 20 or so regulars will be active in a given period. We’re at the beginning, so that’s a direction, not today’s roster.

Is RP required?

No. There’s no required backstory, mandatory in-character time, or guild storyline to study. Enjoy RP at your own depth, and give others room to enjoy theirs.

Are new players welcome?

Absolutely. Curiosity, communication, and a willingness to learn matter more than experience. We’re discovering Monsters & Memories together.

Hardcore or casual?

Competent without being sweaty. We care about playing well together, but Hearthlight isn’t a raid or progression machine. No attendance requirements, no leveling race.

How is the guild run?

The founder sets the broad values, tone, guardrails, and direction. Members have room to start groups, organize activities, and build traditions. Ideas are welcome; every decision doesn’t need a vote.

How do I join?

Start with a hello in the Hearthlight Discord. Tell us a little about yourself and what you enjoy in-game, ask your questions, and see whether the company feels right. Our new Discord is being set up; the invite will be added here when it’s ready.
