THE BEGINNING

Help shape Hearthlight.

Hearthlight is starting alongside Monsters & Memories Early Access, founded in October 2026. We have a clear direction, but the friendships, stories, and traditions are still ahead of us.

The foundation is here.
What gets built on it will be shaped by the people who show up.

Start a group. Suggest an expedition. Share what you’ve learned. Organize some RP. You don’t need a title to get something going.

The founding group is still taking shape. The broad values and guardrails are here; the everyday life, traditions, and culture of Hearthlight will grow from the people who build it together.

LIGHT–MEDIUM RP

A little more life in Eth-ur

RP belongs alongside ordinary play: a few words in character while grouping, a conversation in town, an expedition with an in-world reason, or relationships that develop along the way.

Bring as much character as makes the game more fun for you.

No mandatory RP, required backstory, or in-character hours. No ongoing guild storyline you have to study. There’s room for small hooks, occasional events, and deeper RP between people who want it.

OUR WAYS

Good company, in practice

Real life first.

No attendance requirements and no leveling race. Play when you can, and communicate when plans change.

Be someone people want in the group.

Learn your character, communicate, cooperate, and take feedback well. Respect guildmates and the other people you meet.

Take part when you’re here.

Say hello, ask for a group, invite someone along, or lend a hand. A guild tag should be more than a silent name overhead.

Things will go sideways. Recover, regroup, learn what you can, and keep moving.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions

How big will Hearthlight be?

We’re aiming for around 30–50 members over time, with a smaller core of regulars active during any given stretch. People will naturally come and go. The goal is enough activity to find company, while staying small enough that names remain familiar.

Are new players welcome?

Absolutely. Experience matters less than curiosity, communication, and a willingness to learn. We’re figuring out a new world together.

Hardcore or casual?

Competent without being sweaty. We care about playing well and taking on challenging content, but Hearthlight isn’t a raid or progression machine.

How is the guild run?

Hearthlight is being built by a founding group rather than around one traditional GM. The broad values and guardrails are shared, while members have room to start groups, organize activities, bring RP ideas to life, and help shape traditions. Not every decision needs a vote, and no one person is meant to carry the guild.

How do I join?

Say hello in the Hearthlight Discord. Tell us a little about yourself, ask your questions, and see whether the company feels right. The new Discord invite will appear here when it’s ready.
